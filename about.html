This site

How America Votes maps each state's election results, by land and by votes, so you can see how much of a map's color is empty land and how much is people. It's free, with no ads and no sign-up.

It's my own project. It isn't affiliated with, or endorsed by, any state, election office or campaign. Every result comes from the office that runs the election, and the sources and credits page names each one.
