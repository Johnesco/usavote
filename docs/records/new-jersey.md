## Terms of use, read 2026-11-19

This is a reading of the published pages, not legal advice. Nobody at the Division of Elections has been asked.

| Source | What it says | What it asks of us |
|---|---|---|
| [NJ.gov: Legal Statement & Disclaimers](https://nj.gov/nj/legal.html), the "State of New Jersey Conditions of Use Notice", read in full, linked from the Division of Elections' pages | Section F: "anyone may view, copy or distribute State information found here without obligation to the State, unless otherwise stated on particular material". Other sections: accuracy, links, endorsement, monitoring, the State Seal (restricted by law), and "Unauthorized attempts to modify ... any information stored on the State's web pages" (tampering with the site). It notes that departments "may have additional privacy and use policies". | **Permission to copy and distribute.** Don't use the State Seal or agency logos |
| [Department of State: Legal Notices](https://www.nj.gov/state/dos-legal-notices.shtml) | Notices of public meetings, not terms of use | Nothing |

**The reading:** **permission given.** New Jersey is the only state read so far whose terms say outright that anyone may copy and distribute state information. That covers republishing results; redrawing them as maps isn't named, but nothing limits it. No Division of Elections terms beyond these were found.
