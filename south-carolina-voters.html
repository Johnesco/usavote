Drag to move. Scroll, pinch or double-click to zoom. Spots and colors are estimated. Results are unofficial until the state certifies them.
How?

Each county's total for the race is real. Its voters are placed by how each party's 2026 U.S. Senate vote was spread across the county's precincts, then by where adults lived in 2020. The precincts are the Census Bureau's 2020 voting districts, matched to the 2026 precincts by name; some are combined, and votes in precincts new since 2020 and countywide failsafe and provisional votes are shared out by rule, so a precinct's mix is sometimes an estimate. Zoomed far out, dots stacked on the same pixel are left out, since only the top one could show; closer in, every voter's dot is drawn.

Uses the Census Bureau's TIGER/Line® data, repackaged here. TIGER/Line® is the Census Bureau's registered trademark; this site is not a Census Bureau product.
