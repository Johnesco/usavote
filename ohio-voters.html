Unofficial and partial: ballots are still outstanding. Land grid from TIGER/Line® data, not a Census Bureau product. Drag to move. Scroll, pinch or double-click to zoom. Spots and colors are estimated.
How?

Each county's total for the race is real so far: these are the Ohio Secretary of State's unofficial, partial results, with absentee and provisional ballots still outstanding, so totals will change. Its voters are placed by where adults lived in 2020, with the same mix of parties everywhere in the county. So the dots show how many people voted and roughly where, but not which neighborhoods lean which way. Zoomed far out, dots stacked on the same pixel are left out, since only the top one could show; closer in, every voter's dot is drawn.
