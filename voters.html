Guided flight ▶ Misreading the Map
Drag to move. Scroll, pinch or double-click to zoom. Spots and colors are estimated.
How?

Each county's total for the race is real. Its voters are placed by how each party's 2024 Senate vote was spread across the county's precincts, then by where adults lived in 2020. Zoomed far out, dots stacked on the same pixel are left out, since only the top one could show; closer in, every voter's dot is drawn.
