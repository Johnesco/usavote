Drag to move. Scroll, pinch or double-click to zoom. Spots and colors are estimated. Results are unofficial.
How?

Each county's total for the race is real, though unofficial until the State Canvassing Board declares it. Its voters are placed by where adults lived in 2020, with the same mix of parties everywhere in the county. So the dots show how many people voted and roughly where, but not which neighborhoods lean which way. Zoomed far out, dots stacked on the same pixel are left out, since only the top one could show; closer in, every voter's dot is drawn.

Dots stay off land where the 2020 Census counted no one.
